Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vanza Marketing, a DBA of Vanza Growth LLC ("Vanza," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you visit vanzamarketing.com (the "Site") or contact us. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out our contact form or email us, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, and the details of your message.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the Site, certain information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including your device and browser type, pages you view, how you arrived at the Site, general (city or region level) location, and how you interact with the Site.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior in an anonymized form. This information is processed by Google. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Analytics opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

Most web browsers also let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you about your project
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To understand Site traffic and how our content performs
  • To protect the Site and comply with legal obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the Site and our business (for example, Google for analytics and our email and hosting providers), and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Data retention

We keep the information you provide for as long as needed to respond to you and for our legitimate business purposes, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided to us by emailing sales@vanzamarketing.com. You can also opt out of analytics as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Third-party links

The Site may link to other websites, including our clients' sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us at sales@vanzamarketing.com or (619) 857-0501.
