VANZA Marketing is an independent digital agency based in San Diego, California. We design websites and run search strategies for small businesses across the country — and we've done it our own way since day one.

Who We Are

A small agency, built around the work.

VANZA Marketing was founded in 2022 with a simple idea: small businesses deserve the same quality of digital marketing that big companies get, without the bloated retainers, the slow turnaround, or the language nobody understands.

We're independently owned, self-funded, and intentionally lean. That means when you work with us, you're working with the people actually doing the work — not a sales rep in one office and a contractor in another. The person you talk to is the person who owns your project.

Today we serve small businesses across professional services and the trades, but the model hasn't changed: clear conversations, focused execution, and websites and SEO that actually move the needle.

The VANZA Vision

Be the most trusted digital partner for the small businesses we serve — by doing fewer things, exceptionally well, and treating every client like the most important one we have.

What We Stand For

Core values that guide our work.

These aren't slogans — they're the actual standards we measure ourselves against. If we're falling short on any of them, we want to hear about it.

Do the right thing

If we wouldn't recommend it to a friend, we don't sell it to a client. The fastest way to lose trust is to cash a check we shouldn't have taken.

Show up, every time

Calls returned same-day. Deadlines met as promised. Updates sent without being asked. Reliability is the rarest thing in this industry — and the one we refuse to compromise on.

Keep getting better

Search algorithms change. Customer behavior changes. Design trends change. We never stop learning — and we bring what we learn straight back to client work.

Sweat the details

Anyone can build a website. Building one that loads fast, ranks well, looks sharp on every screen, and converts visitors into leads — that's a different job. The details aren't optional.

Talk straight

No jargon, no hedging, no overpromising. If we don't know, we say so. If something isn't working, we tell you and fix it. Marketing should be the most clearly-explained service you buy.

Treat every client like the only one

Whether you're our biggest account or our smallest, you get the same level of attention, the same person to call, and the same standard of work. That's non-negotiable.

Our Mission

Help small businesses win their corner of the internet.

When a customer searches for what you sell, you should be one of the first names they see — and when they land on your site, it should be obvious within 10 seconds that you're the right call.

That's the entire job. Not vanity metrics, not impressions, not chasing trends nobody actually cares about. Just helping real businesses get more of the right customers, more often, more predictably.

We want to be the agency our clients refer their friends to without hesitation. Everything we build is in service of that.

— The VANZA Team
What a Great Agency Looks Like

The standard we hold ourselves to.

01

Clients refer us without being asked

The truest measure of whether we're doing the job right. If our clients aren't recommending us to their network, something's broken.

02

We do the things that don't scale

Picking up the phone. Walking through a strategy in plain English. Catching small mistakes before they become big ones. The unglamorous work is the work.

03

We say no when we should

If a client wants something we don't think will work, we say so — even when it costs us the upsell. A great agency protects clients from bad decisions, including their own.

04

The work speaks for itself

Sites that load fast, rank well, and convert. Profiles that bring in calls. Numbers that go up. Marketing that's measurable and worth what it costs.

05

People want to be on the team

A great company is a great place to work. Talent matters, and the only way to keep good people is to build something worth being part of.

06

It outlasts trends

The internet will keep changing. Algorithms, design fashions, platforms — all of it. A great agency is built to keep delivering value after the current trend dies.
